The Gap

Why not just drink spring or purified water?

Most bottled water asks you to choose between clean and mineral-rich. Spring water carries natural minerals but reflects whatever is happening around its source, so it changes over time. Distilled and reverse-osmosis water is clean but empty. Peak Hydration is purified completely, then rebuilt — so it is both.

Water Purity Minerals Consistency bottle to bottle
Spring water Reflects the surrounding environment — geology, rainfall, farming, mining, nearby development Naturally present Mineral content can change over time
Municipal tap water Treated at source, then travels through decades-old infrastructure Present, but variable Varies by area and pipe condition
Distilled & reverse-osmosis water Very clean Stripped out — no minerals, no electrolytes Consistent
